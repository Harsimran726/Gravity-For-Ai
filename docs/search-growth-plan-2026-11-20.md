## Implemented foundation
Brand alias and Google entity link in shared Organization/WebSite markup; visible brand explanation; revised homepage and service headings; buying-intent service links; private AI service page with visible answers and matching schema; www canonical redirect; removal of AMP alternatives from canonical sitemap; removal of repeated title suffix. Existing city coverage retained. No mass generation of near-identical city/service pages.

## Days 1–7: establish measurement and local accuracy
Verify the domain property in Search Console, submit /sitemap.xml, inspect home and all four services, and request indexing through URL Inspection where available. Record page-indexing exclusions and Google's chosen canonicals. The site's existing Google Indexing API widget is not the supported submission method for ordinary service/blog pages; use Search Console instead.

In GBP, verify ownership and choose the closest available primary category to the actual main business (website designer if web projects are the primary business; otherwise the accurate software/IT category). Add accurate secondary categories, service descriptions, contact details, opening hours and website. Use the public business name Gravity For AI; do not add keyword or city strings to it. Use Mansa as the real base, hide a residential address if customers are not received there, and set only genuine service areas. Do not create remote-city listings.

Add a tagged website URL: https://gravityforai.com/?utm_source=google&utm_medium=organic&utm_campaign=business_profile . Confirm analytics receives the visit and tracks contact submissions as qualified leads. Test the contact form before sending traffic. Connect Search Console and analytics access for ongoing measurement; access is currently outstanding.

## Days 8–30: prove relevance and trust
Publish two permission-backed project stories showing the customer's starting problem, implementation, measured outcome, dates and limitations. Audit existing testimonials and performance claims against evidence before promoting them. Request honest reviews from actual customers after delivery, without incentives or scripts that dictate keywords. Update real company social profiles to use consistent name, canonical domain and Mansa location. Add only verified company profiles to sameAs markup.

Improve existing Mansa, Bathinda, Ludhiana and Chandigarh pages with genuine delivery details and local examples. Link naturally to relevant service pages. Avoid claiming physical offices in service areas. Build one useful decision guide weekly: website project cost factors; private LLM versus managed API; AI agent pilot scope; missed-call workflow setup. Link each guide to its primary service with a relevant CTA.

## Days 31–60: grow qualified demand
Use Search Console queries to refine headings and answer buyer questions that actually generate impressions. Compare mobile engagement and lead quality by service and region. Expand the best converting topic with a technical demonstration, deployment checklist or real case study. Prioritize partnerships and legitimate local/industry listings that real buyers use; track referral leads. Do not buy bulk backlinks.

## Days 61–90: scale a proven market
Choose one international segment based on pipeline evidence, scope clarity and ability to support working hours. Improve its existing coverage page with real delivery process, contracts, communication and relevant proof. Create distinct industry pages only when offering and evidence justify them. Review pages receiving impressions but few clicks, and pages receiving clicks but no qualified enquiries.

## AEO/GEO implementation principle
Use crawlable HTML, explicit business/service identity, concise answers, source-backed case studies, stable URLs and contextual internal links. Schema must describe visible content. FAQ markup does not promise a Google FAQ rich result or AI citation. No special AI schema or llms.txt file is required for Google's AI features.

## Weekly scorecard
Measure brand query impressions/clicks, nonbrand commercial query impressions/clicks, indexed canonical service pages, qualified enquiries, booked calls and won revenue by landing page and geography. Measure GBP calls and website visits separately. Use a consistent location/device for manual rank samples; an unlocalized search is not a local ranking report. Set numeric growth targets after collecting a 28-day baseline. Report meaningful changes and experiments, not invented ranking gains.
