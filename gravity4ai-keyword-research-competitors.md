### 1.2 Category-level / platform competitors (what a prospect finds first when they Google "AI voice agent India")
These aren't local agencies — they're SaaS platforms — but they shape what your prospect *expects* to see and compare you against:
`Vapi, Retell AI, Bland AI, Bolna, Gnani.ai, SquadStack, Caller Digital, Vomyra, EchoLeads, Haptik, VaniAgent`

**What this means for your positioning:** most of these are self-serve tools or developer platforms — a local business owner in Bathinda or Ludhiana isn't going to configure Vapi themselves. Your actual competitive edge in copy should be explicit: **"done-for-you, not a DIY platform you have to configure."** This is a real, defensible line other Chandigarh agencies aren't saying clearly either — most of them also read like tech-feature lists rather than "we set this up and manage it for you."

### 1.3 Competitor content pattern (what to do differently)
- They lead with **credentials** (years, client count). You lead with the **prospect's problem** (missed calls, wasted hours) — per your 7-step framework. This alone differentiates the page structure.
- None of them show a clear 3-step "how it works to hire us" process — most jump straight to a contact form. Your Plan section is a genuine UX/trust advantage.
- Pricing: IT Marketz shows bands, others hide pricing entirely. Showing at least a starting price or clear tiers (Part 8 of your framework) beats full opacity for both conversion and AEO ("how much does X cost" questions).

## PART 2 — Core Keyword Architecture (SEO + AEO + GEO)

Same 4-field method as the H4Ai research: 🟢 transactional (title/H1/CTA) · 🟡 commercial investigation (H2/body) · 🔵 informational/AEO question (FAQ, feeds schema) · GEO seed (the quotable direct-answer paragraph).

- 🟢 `AI voice agent for local business [city]`

- 🟡 `AI voice agent vs hiring a receptionist`
- 🟡 `AI calling agent pricing India`

## PART 5 — GEO checklist (apply site-wide)
- Every page's Hero + Value Proposition sections together must form a standalone, quotable 40–60 word answer to "what does Gravity For AI do."
- Never define "agentic AI" or "AI voice agent" differently across pages — one canonical definition, reused verbatim in the Explanatory Paragraph sections.
- Add `FAQPage` schema to every FAQ block, `Service` schema to every landing page, `Organization` schema site-wide.
- Since the brand name itself won't be searched early (see overview file), GEO citation depends entirely on category-question strength — invest FAQ/explanatory-paragraph quality over brand-name repetition.
