# Gravity For AI — City-Based Landing Pages
Shorter version of the 7-step framework (Header/Footer shared site-wide, so each city page needs Hero → Stakes → Value Prop → Guide → Plan → Price, condensed). Roll out in the priority order below — do not publish all 13 at once (thin/duplicate-content risk, same warning as the H4Ai plan).

## Template (`/locations/[city]`)

**[TITLE]** `{Primary Service} for {City} Businesses | Gravity For AI`
**[META]** `Gravity For AI provides {service(s)} for businesses in {City}. {One local differentiator}. Book a free AI audit.`
**[H1]** `{City}'s AI Automation & Website Partner` *(or lead with the city's #1 priority service if one dominates — e.g. Chandigarh should lead with Agentic AI/Voice Agents given the competitor landscape, not generic "web development")*

**Hero subheadline:** One sentence stating you serve this city specifically + the core benefit.

**Stakes (condensed to 2–3 bullets, localized):** Reuse the homepage Stakes bullets, but pick the 2 most relevant to that city's dominant business type (e.g., manufacturing/export businesses in Ludhiana → lead with "missed inquiry calls," not "salon no-shows").

**Value Proposition:** Same core paragraph as homepage, with the city name inserted naturally — do not rewrite the whole thing per city, that's where thin-content risk comes from; the city-specific parts are Stakes and the closing line below.

**The Guide:** Local testimonial if you have one from that city; otherwise, general stats.

**The Plan:** Identical 3-step process, unchanged — consistency here is good, not a duplicate-content risk (process pages are expected to repeat).

**Price:** Link to the relevant service landing page's pricing table rather than repeating it.

**[SCHEMA]** `Service` + `areaServed: {City}` for every page except Mansa, which gets full `LocalBusiness` schema (your real, verifiable address).

**⚠️ Uniqueness requirement (repeat of the earlier warning, still critical):** each page needs at least one genuinely city-specific sentence — a real local detail, not just the city name swapped in a template. Budget actual writing time per page; publish 2–3/month, not all at once.

## Rollout order (from the competitive-density findings in the keyword file)

**Wave 1 — low competition, fast wins (build these first):**
- Mansa *(your real HQ — build this one with full LocalBusiness schema and the most local detail of any page)*

**Wave 2 — Punjab tier-1, still winnable:**

**Wave 3 — high competition, needs proof points first (don't rush these):**

- Noida
- New Delhi

**Wave 4 — remote-sell markets, lower priority initially:**
- Pune
- Surat
- Hyderabad
